\section*{1 Linear systems}

Consider the system $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ with
\[
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 0 \\ -1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
The matrix $A$ is symmetric and positive definite.
