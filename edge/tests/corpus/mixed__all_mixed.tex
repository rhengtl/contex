We begin with the printed definition of velocity.
\[
v = u + at
\]
Then the displacement follows by integration.
\[
s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2
\]
Finally the kinetic energy is written as
\[
E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2
\]
