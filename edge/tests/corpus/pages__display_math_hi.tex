\section*{1 Transforms}

The Fourier transform of $f$ is
\begin{equation}
    F(\omega) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t) e^{-i\omega t} \, dt.
\end{equation}

Its inverse recovers the original signal:
\begin{equation}
    f(t) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} F(\omega) e^{i\omega t} \, d\omega.
\end{equation}
