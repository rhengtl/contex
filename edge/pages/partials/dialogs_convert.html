If you continue without AI

Your document can still be converted, in this browser rather than on any server — nothing is sent anywhere — but the result will be of lower quality. In particular:

  • complex document structure may not be reconstructed accurately;
  • unclear or difficult handwriting may produce poor results;
  • mathematics is more likely to be misread;
  • it downloads about 50 MB the first time, and is slower.

Do not expect the same quality as an AI-powered conversion.

{# THE CAMERA PICKER IS NOT IN THE FLASK ORIGINAL. That version had only Flip, which toggles facingMode between "environment" and "user" -- a phone's two cameras. On a desktop the operating system decides what "environment" means, and Windows will hand over a phone paired through Phone Link in preference to the webcam, with nothing on screen saying so or offering the other one. This lists the cameras by name and lets one be chosen outright. Flip stays, because on a phone it is one tap and the list is three. It moves to the next camera in this list when there is a list, and falls back to toggling facingMode when there is not.
