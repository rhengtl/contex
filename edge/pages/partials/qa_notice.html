AI conversion is not set up on this server

GEMINI_API_KEY is missing, so documents will be converted in this browser instead — nothing is sent anywhere, but the results are of lower quality. You will be asked first.

Before you upload

Your document is sent to Google's Gemini API to be read. This server is on the free tier, and Google uses free-tier input to train its models — so please do not upload anything confidential, personal or sensitive.

If that service is unavailable, the fallback OCR runs in this browser and sends nothing anywhere. You are always asked first.
