If you continue without AI

Your document can still be converted on this server, but the result will be of lower quality. In particular:

  • complex document structure may not be reconstructed accurately;
  • unclear or difficult handwriting may produce poor results;
  • mathematics is more likely to be misread;
  • Word documents are converted directly and lose only LaTeX polish.

Do not expect the same quality as an AI-powered conversion.
