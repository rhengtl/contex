4. What ConTeX does

ConTeX converts a document you provide into LaTeX source (a .tex file) and renders that source to a PDF preview. You can provide a document as:

  • an image file (PNG, JPG, JPEG, BMP, TIFF, WebP or GIF);
  • a PDF file;
  • a Microsoft Word document (.docx);
  • a photograph taken with your device camera through the Service; or
  • handwriting or drawing you make on the in-app canvas.

Conversion normally works by sending your document to a third-party AI service, which reads it and produces the LaTeX. Word documents are sent as extracted text rather than as a file. Where that service is unavailable, ConTeX can instead convert your document using software running on its own server. Section 6 and the Privacy Policy explain where your document goes in each case.

5. Accuracy is not guaranteed

Conversion is performed by optical character recognition and by AI language models. Both make mistakes. ConTeX is not suitable as the sole record of anything that matters, and must not be relied on for any purpose where an error would cause harm, loss, or an incorrect decision.
